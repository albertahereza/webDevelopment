Project Summary

Makerere University has a wide range of student clubs and organizations that promote leadership, networking, learning, and personal development[cite: 1]. The proposed solution is the development of MUK Connect, a smart mobile application that centralizes information about officially recognized student clubs while helping students plan their daily activities[cite: 1].

The application will allow users to browse clubs, apply for membership, register for events, create personal schedules, and receive personalized recommendations for club events and campus activities that fit into their free time without causing scheduling conflicts[cite: 1].

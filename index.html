Project Summary

Makerere University has a wide range of student clubs and organizations that promote leadership, networking, learning, and personal development[cite: 1]. However, information about these clubs and their activities is scattered across notice boards, social media platforms, and messaging groups, making it difficult for many students, especially new students, to discover available opportunities[cite: 1]. In addition, students often struggle to balance their academic responsibilities with extracurricular activities because they lack a simple way to organize their schedules[cite: 1].

The proposed solution is the development of MUK Connect, a smart mobile application that centralizes information about officially recognized student clubs while helping students plan their daily activities[cite: 1]. The application will allow users to browse clubs, apply for membership, register for events, create personal schedules, and receive personalized recommendations for club events and campus activities that fit into their free time without causing scheduling conflicts[cite: 1].

Project Details & Explanations

1. System Architecture & Problem Context

Right now, students struggle to find information about clubs because club details are scattered across WhatsApp groups, Facebook pages, and email[cite: 2]. There is no central place where students can see all clubs and events[cite: 2]. MUK Connect brings together a list of all student clubs at Makerere University, information about club activities, and a personal schedule tool to plan study time[cite: 2].

2. Target Audience

The platform is designed for two main groups of users[cite: 1]:

  • Student Users: Regular university students who want to find clubs, see events, and manage their time[cite: 2].
  • Club Administrators: Leaders of student clubs who manage club information, create events, and approve students who want to join[cite: 2].

3. Technology & Methodology

The methodology involves developing the application using Flutter for cross-platform mobile development and Firebase for user authentication, cloud database management, and real-time notifications[cite: 1]. The app needs internet access to work and connects to Firebase servers in the cloud to store data[cite: 2].

[ Visual Diagram 3: Tech Stack Overview ]

Illustration showing Flutter communicating with Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)[cite: 2].

4. Core Functionalities

MUK Connect allows students and club leaders to perform several key activities[cite: 2]:

  • Account Management: Create an account, log in, and manage profile information[cite: 2].
  • Browse & Join Clubs: See a list of all clubs, search for clubs, view club details, and request to join[cite: 2].
  • Events Management: See all club events with dates, times, and locations, and register to attend[cite: 2].
  • Personal Schedule Management: Create personal schedule items, view schedules in a calendar, and automatically add registered events[cite: 2].

5. Expected Benefits & Non-Functional Requirements

The expected benefits include improving access to club information, enhancing students' time management, increasing participation in campus activities, reducing scheduling conflicts, strengthening communication, and creating a more connected university community[cite: 1].

The app must meet strict performance and security goals, such as starting in less than 3 seconds, encrypting all passwords, and ensuring students can only see their own personal information[cite: 2].
